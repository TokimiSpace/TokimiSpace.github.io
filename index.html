模組化雙 ESP32-S3 遙控車原型，結合瀏覽器遙控、獨立 OV3660 攝影節點、OLED 動畫表情與 32 顆可定址燈光。Supercar V3 車殼的可編輯 Blender 檔與列印資料也已公開；其 195 × 100 mm 孔距尚未和歷史 203 × 105 mm 實車紀錄完成實體核對。 A modular dual-ESP32-S3 rover prototype with browser teleoperation, an independent OV3660 camera node, animated OLED faces, and 32-pixel addressable lighting. The editable Blender file and print package for the Supercar V3 top cover are also public; its 195 × 100 mm pattern has not been physically reconciled with the historical 203 × 105 mm rover record.

PUBLISHING PROTOCOL / 04

開源，不省略邊界。 Open, with the boundaries intact.

  1. A

    可驗證 Verifiable

    區分原始碼確認、建置確認、實機紀錄與未來規劃。 We distinguish source-confirmed, build-confirmed, historically observed, and planned work.

  2. B

    限制透明 Transparent limits

    公開已知問題，不把原型包裝成成品。 Known limitations stay visible; prototypes are not presented as finished products.

  3. C

    授權清楚 Clear licensing

    程式、硬體來源與文件依專案標示各自授權；品牌權利維持獨立。 Code, hardware source, and documentation carry scoped licenses; brand rights remain separate.
